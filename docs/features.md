# Features

Everything the island does, and where to find it.

| Feature | What you see | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drag actions** | Drag a file in **any app** (your file manager, the desktop, a browser's downloads) and hold still for a moment: a ring of six actions blooms under the pointer, **Convert**, **Edit**, **Capture**, **Ask Claude**, **Shrink** and **Copy text**, always in the same places. Actions that don't fit the file are dimmed. Pointing at Convert or Ask fans out a second ring of formats or questions | Let go on an action and it runs; the island shows it working, then the new file (**Open**, **Show in folder**, **Undo**, or drag it straight out of the island into an email or chat). Convert PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, Markdown, text and images (PNG, JPEG, WebP) to other formats, make a **smaller copy** of a photo or PDF, copy all of a document's text, copy an image, or ask Claude to summarize, translate or explain it. **Alt** opens the ring at once. Move away, or let go in the middle, and the drag carries on as usual. Linux (X11), macOS and Windows |
| **Connected (trading)** | Your [Day-Trading](https://github.com/Shashanksharma280201/Day-Trading) engine on the island: today's P&L on the capsule (green up, red down), each trade it proposes as a card with what, how much, the stop, the target, the risk and a countdown, and a **Trading** tab with open positions, today's trades with charges, and **Stop trading today** / **Close everything now** | Start the engine, then paste its address and token in **Settings → Connected**. Answer a card with its buttons or the approval shortcuts; no answer means no trade. Pair your phone from the same section (**Show QR**) |
| **Services** | The **Services** tab: GitHub (pull requests to review, yours with their CI), Vercel (deployments), Stripe (balance and payments), Resend (emails), Notion (recently edited pages), Cal.com (upcoming calls) and n8n (workflow runs). A heads-up pops up on the island for a failed deploy, CI breaking on your PR, a review request, a new payment or a failed workflow, and waits in Notifications | Turn each on and paste its key in **Settings → Services** (a wrong key is refused, with why). Click a row to open it on the service's site |
| **Weekly recap** | Every Monday, last week with your coding agents: time, sessions, files, lines, commands, your top agent and project, the busiest day | **Share image** makes a picture of the week (with your character) to save to Pictures or copy; **Hide project names** leaves them out. Any time from the tray menu or Settings |
| **Wardrobe and desktop companion** | 13 outfits plus a **Seasonal** one that changes with the date (a witch hat at Halloween, a Santa hat in December…), and a body colour. Turn on **On your desktop too** and the character also sits on your desktop, acting out what's going on and dozing when it's quiet | Pick an outfit and colour in **Settings → Character**, or right-click the resting island. Drag the desktop character anywhere (it remembers the spot); click it to open the island. Put your own sound files (done.mp3, ask.wav…) in the sounds folder (Settings → General → Sounds → Open Folder) to replace the built-in tones |
| **Character** | The island's agent has a face: pick **Orbit** (the orb, with a face), **Bolt** (a little robot) or **Puff** (a soft dumpling) in **Settings → Character** and give it a name. It acts out what the agent is doing: listening, thinking, searching, writing, working, done, needs you, something went wrong, and it sleeps on the resting capsule late at night. A coloured ring around it shows the mood (spinning while it works, breathing when it needs you), its eyes follow your pointer anywhere on screen, and it says hello once a day | Click a character in Settings to switch; the previews act out every mood. In the Claude tab, boop it; boop it three times quickly and it gets dizzy. Rest the pointer on it and it shows some love |
| **Edge or top capsule** | A slim black capsule docked to the right or left edge of the screen, or **at the top center, below the camera**, as a horizontal pill that grows downward like a phone's Dynamic Island. With music playing it shows the album art and a waveform | Rest the pointer on it and it **peeks**: one line about what's going on (Claude's current step, the song playing); keep resting and the full card opens. Click it (or press **Ctrl+I** anywhere) to open the panel. **Drag it** up or down the edge, across the screen to the other side, or up to the top center; it remembers the spot. Or pick **Left / Top / Right** in Settings → General |
| **Now Playing** | Album art + animated waveform while music plays in any MPRIS player: Spotify (app or web), YouTube / YouTube Music in Chrome or Firefox, VLC, Rhythmbox, … | Hover to expand: title, artist, progress bar (click it to seek), previous / play-pause / next, shuffle and repeat |
| **Panel (Controls / Chats / Mail / Notes / Documents / CRM)** | Opens on click or **Ctrl+I**, with a slim column of icons floating beside it (like a detached dock) that remembers the last section. The Chats and Mail icons show a red badge with your unread count, and the Claude icon has two rings showing your session (outer) and weekly (inner) plan usage, turning orange at 80% and red at 95% | Click an icon to switch between the Control Center, your WhatsApp chats, your mail inbox, your notes, your documents and your CRM at any time, not only when something new arrives. The gear at the bottom opens Settings. **Ctrl+I** again (or moving the pointer away) closes it |
| **WhatsApp** | New messages pop up as a card per chat. **Chats** lists your recent chats with coloured avatars, a one-line preview, time and unread count, plus a search field. Conversations show bubbles with times, "Today / Yesterday" dividers and coloured sender names in groups. Photos and videos show as pictures (tap for full size), voice notes play right there, and files open with one tap (saved to Downloads) | Reply from the card or from the conversation view, search chats, **Mark as Read**, **Open Chats** |
| **Notes** | Your notes, newest first, with a search field | **+** starts a new note (ready to type), click one to open and edit it, the trash icon deletes it. Notes save automatically as you type, as plain Markdown files on this computer |
| **Mail** | New mail pops up as a card. **Mail** lists your recent inbox (unread dot, sender, subject, preview, time), with an account picker when you have several | Open a message to read it (marks it read, like Mail), **Reply** (threaded, saved to Sent), **Mark as Read**, **Open Inbox** |
| **Two activities at once** | The capsule plus a small detached circle below it for the second activity | Hover to expand the main one |
| **Spotify** | The **Music** tab, styled like Spotify: Home (greeting, Liked Songs and what you played recently, your playlists), Search, Your Library, playlist and album pages whose header takes the cover's colour, a mini player and a full Now Playing screen. While Spotify plays, the pop-out Now Playing card uses Spotify's colours too | Play any song inside its playlist or album, like / unlike, shuffle, repeat, seek, next / previous. If nothing is playing anywhere it uses one of your Spotify devices or the Spotify app on this computer; otherwise **Connect to a device** lets you pick your phone or play in your browser (the Spotify Web Player). The device button in the player moves playback between devices |
| **Claude: plan limits** | Your 5-hour session and weekly usage as two meters, with when each resets. A heads-up card when you pass 80% and 95% | Works with Claude Pro and Max. Turn on **Show my plan limits** in Settings (or the button in the Claude tab) |
| **Any AI** | Pick the AI behind the agent in **Settings → AI**: Claude Code, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible service | Paste your key; the island checks it and lists the models |
| **Packages** | What the agent can do: Notes, WhatsApp, Mail, Music, Documents and CRM, each with its own tab, turned on or off in **Settings → Packages** | Ask “catch me up on my chats”, “reply to Rahul that I'm running late”, “play some lofi”. Anything that sends something asks you first |
| **Documents** | **Drop files on the island** (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, PowerPoint, Markdown, text) and they appear in the **Documents** tab, newest first, with pages and sizes. Files made from them are marked and saved in `~/Documents/Dynamic Island`; your originals are never changed | Without any AI: **merge** PDFs, keep / delete / reorder **pages**, **rotate**, **split**, **number pages**, **convert** (Word / Excel / Markdown / text to PDF, CSV ↔ Excel, anything to text). Or ask the agent: “fix the spelling and grammar” (it marks its corrections as **tracked changes** you accept or reject in Word or LibreOffice), “summarize this”, “compare these two versions”, “add a DRAFT watermark”, “write a cover letter as a Word file”. Save a request you use often as a **recipe** for one tap next time |
| **CRM** | Your own simple CRM in the **CRM** tab: **People** (search, Lead / Customer / Partner, when you last talked), each person's page (details, follow-ups, deals and a timeline of calls, meetings and notes), **Deals** (your pipeline and its total, by stage) and **Follow-ups** (overdue, today, upcoming). All of it is stored on this computer | Works fully without AI: add and edit people, log a call or note, add follow-ups with due dates (a card pops up on the island when one is due), move deals through the stages, import contacts from **Google Contacts, Outlook, Excel or a phone's vCard**, export to CSV. Or let the agent run it: “log a call with Rahul, he wants a quote, remind me Friday”, “who should I follow up with this week?”, “move the Acme deal to won”. What the agent changes shows in a bar with **Undo**, and deleting asks you first |
| **Claude: talk or type to Claude Code** | The **Claude** tab: a conversation with Claude Code in the project folder you pick. Your character shows what it's doing: listening, working out what you said, thinking, searching, editing, writing | Tap the mic (or press **Ctrl+Alt+Space** anywhere), say what you want, and stop talking: it's transcribed on your computer and sent to Claude Code. Or type it. Follow-ups continue the same conversation; **Stop** ends a run; the ✎ button starts a new conversation. The paperclip attaches a screenshot of the window you opened the island from (X11), so you can ask "what's wrong here?". When the panel is closed, the character on the capsule shows Claude working with a step count; rest the pointer on it to see the current step roll by, and a card pops up with the answer |
| **Claude Code sessions** | Every Claude Code session in your terminals, live: while one works, the capsule shows your character with an orb for what it's doing (searching, editing, running) and a step count (or how many sessions are working). Rest the pointer on it for the current step. When a session needs you, a card says so; when it finishes, a card shows its last answer, how long it took and what it changed (**+12 −3**, which files) | **Open terminal** brings that session's terminal (or editor) to the front. The **Sessions** tab lists every session with its status, time and changes; open one for its steps and a **diff of every file** it touched. The finished card stays quiet if you're already looking at that terminal. **Codex, Gemini CLI and Cursor Agent** sessions show the same way: turn each on in Settings → Claude |
| **Claude asks, plans** | Claude's questions (`AskUserQuestion`) as a card, one question at a time with its options and their descriptions; its plan in full before it starts | Pick an option (several for multi-select), or type your own answer under **Something else**, then **Send**; it goes straight back to Claude. From any app: **Ctrl+Alt+1…9** picks, **Ctrl+Alt+Y** moves on or sends. For a plan: **Start** or **Keep planning**. **Answer in terminal** leaves either to the terminal |
| **Claude Code approvals** | When Claude Code needs permission, the island expands with the tool, the exact command / file / diff, and the working directory | **Allow**, **Deny**, **Always allow** (saves Claude's suggested rule, e.g. `Bash(npm test:*)`), or **Answer in terminal**. Or press **Ctrl+Alt+Y** (allow), **Ctrl+Alt+N** (deny) or **Ctrl+Alt+A** (always) from any app: the island never takes your keyboard, so a stray key can't approve anything. Several waiting requests are answered in order, with a `+N` badge and the next cards peeking out below |
| **Desktop notifications** | Every app's notifications appear on the island with the app icon; critical ones get a red outline and stay longer. When several are waiting, a pill under the card says how many | Click (or **×**) to dismiss; **Clear all** on the pill clears every card at once, with **Undo**. For apps that use GNOME's notification API, their buttons (e.g. "Open log", "Reply") appear and work |
| **Notifications tab** | Everything that arrived (app notifications, WhatsApp and mail), grouped by app, newest first, with how long ago; a red badge on the bell counts what you haven't seen. Kept on this computer between restarts | **×** or swipe left to dismiss one, clear one app, or **Clear all** (with **Undo**). Click a chat or mail to open it. **Do Not Disturb** (here, in the Control Center or the tray) keeps cards from popping up: they wait in the list, and the resting island shows a moon with how many are waiting |
| **Control Center** | macOS-style modules: round Wi-Fi / Bluetooth toggles and large Display / Sound sliders | Drag the sliders, click the volume value to mute, toggle Wi-Fi / Bluetooth, open Settings. Closes by itself shortly after the cursor leaves |
| **Dictate anywhere** | Press **Ctrl+Alt+D** in any app and speak; a pill under the island shows your voice | What you said is typed into the app you're in (with Ctrl+Shift+V in terminals) when you stop talking or press the keys again. Recognised on this computer with Whisper, like the Claude tab's voice |
| **Which screen** | The island on your main display, on whichever display has the pointer (it follows you), or on a monitor you pick | **Settings → General → Island lives on**. A picked monitor is found again after you replug it |
| **Tour and shortcuts** | **Show me around** (offered the first time, then in the tray menu and Settings) walks you through the island with example cards. **Shortcuts** in the Control Center lists every shortcut as it's set now | Change any shortcut in **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts**: click it and press the new keys (keys already in use are refused, with why). Cards that close by themselves show a thin countdown that stops while you hover; the pin keeps a card open. **Open on hover** in Settings → General opens the panel without a click |
| **Settings window** | Opened from the tray menu or the Control Center, styled like System Settings | Appearance (Glass / Solid), frosted glass on/off, sounds on/off (soft tones, off by default), island position, open the notes folder, Ctrl+I shortcut on/off, link WhatsApp, add mail accounts, and the general toggles |
| **Tray menu** | An icon in the top bar | Settings, Do Not Disturb, drag actions on/off, show notifications on/off, Claude Code approvals on/off, Start at login on/off, Quit |

Other details:

- Works on HiDPI (scaled) displays and multi-monitor setups; the island sits on
  the primary monitor (or the one you choose with `DI_DISPLAY`) and follows
  resolution or monitor changes.
- Only one copy runs at a time.
- Controls for tools that aren't installed (e.g. no Bluetooth adapter) are hidden
  instead of showing wrong values.
- The look follows Apple's design language: dark HUD "glass" material with a
  light rim, system colours (blue for the main action), capsule buttons,
  iMessage-style reply field and message bubbles, SF Symbols-style icons, and
  the Inter typeface (bundled).
- **Frosted glass**: the island blurs what's behind it, like macOS. On KDE
  Plasma the compositor does this live. Elsewhere on X11 (GNOME, Xfce, …) the
  island takes a snapshot of the screen area behind it while it is collapsed,
  every few seconds, and shows it blurred; the snapshot stays in memory and is
  never saved or sent anywhere. Turn it off with **Frosted glass** in Settings,
  or choose **Solid** for an opaque look. Not available on Wayland.
- Cards open inward from the edge the island is docked to and always stay fully
  on screen, even when the island sits near the top or bottom.
- Cards stay open while you hover them or type a reply, then close by themselves.
- **Ctrl+I** is a global shortcut: while the island runs, other apps no longer
  receive Ctrl+I (for example italics in editors). Turn it off in Settings if
  that gets in the way. It works on X11; Wayland doesn't allow global shortcuts
  for apps.
- The island only takes keyboard focus while you're typing (a reply, a search,
  a note), so it never steals focus from the app you're using.

CHAPTER FIVE
Why: time

The year I made my first million was the year my mother died of cancer.

Within three years, my father was gone too. Cancer too. And sitting in that hospital, the question that would not leave me alone was: what was all this success actually buying me, if not more of this? More time. More presence. More of the only thing that was actually running out.

All that success meant nothing in that room.

Since then, every business I build has to pass one test: the business feeds the life, not the other way around. I have three kids now (maybe one more next year...). Millions with no time for them is pointless to me. I am obsessed with leverage for exactly one reason: producing more without multiplying my hours, so the hours go where they cannot be replaced.

So understand what I am actually chasing with all this AI. Not a cool tech stack. Hours. Presence. The parts of life only I can live.

Keep that test in mind for the rest of this guide. Every piece of the system exists to pass it.

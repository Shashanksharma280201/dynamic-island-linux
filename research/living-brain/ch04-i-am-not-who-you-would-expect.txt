CHAPTER FOUR
I am not who you would expect

I have never written a line of code. Not one.

My first venture, at sixteen, was sales in a company that later turned out to be a Ponzi scheme. When it collapsed, it took everything I had. I rebuilt from zero the only way I knew how: marketing. Funnels, webinars, ads, offers. I got good at it. Good enough to build my own performance marketing agency, Ascend, which I've run for eight years. Good enough to make my first million, and more...

So when AI arrived, I did not come to it as a programmer. I came to it as a business owner who had already been burned once by betting on the wrong machine, and who had rebuilt by learning what actually lasts.

2021: my GPT-3 beta access, before any of this was cool.

And I did not jump on AI because it was trendy. Every marketer is an AI guru now. I am not that. In 2021, before any of this was cool, I applied for beta access to OpenAI's GPT-3, back when you had to write to them and explain what you wanted to build. I have been quietly working with this technology since before ChatGPT existed. What you are reading is not a hype reaction. It is half a decade of watching this thing grow up.

But none of that is the real reason I build the way I build.

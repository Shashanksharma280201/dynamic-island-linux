CHAPTER SIX
The two moments

Two moments took me all in.

The first was December 2022, the ChatGPT moment. Everyone suddenly talked about AI. And it was impressive, honestly. But be precise about what it was: a smarter search box. It sat there waiting for you to type. Everything it wrote, you still had to take away, check, and do something with. A brilliant intern who never leaves the chair. That is why your first experience with AI probably underwhelmed you. You met a chatbot, and a chatbot still needs you to drive. It was revolutionary nonetheless. We started applying AI to every part of my company's processes. But everything still relied heavily on humans.

The second was early 2026. To me it was the Clawdbot moment (later renamed OpenClaw). OpenClaw is an AI agent that works like a digital employee. It does not wait to be asked. It can take over your computer, read your files, use your tools, take a job, go away, do the work, and come back with it finished. That is the word "agentic" in plain language: a chatbot answers you; an agent acts for you. The difference between asking a friend how to fix your sink, and a plumber showing up.

My initial excitement when I gave my agent a Gmail account and it onboarded itself.
I put Sonny, my first AI agent, into my company Slack and told it to read our onboarding playbook like any other employee... and it onboarded itself.

The moment AI could act on its own, it stopped being software to me. It became a workforce.

OpenClaw itself became less relevant today, but it triggered a new wave in AI. The Agentic Wave. I started learning how to build agents, and here is the part nobody tells you: you do not even need to learn how to code anymore. Claude or ChatGPT can help you set it up!

CHAPTER EIGHT
Brilliant, and forgetful

I set up agents everywhere.

One agent doing content. One doing research. One doing follow-ups. One watching numbers. Each one, on its own, was genuinely brilliant. Better than I expected.

And my company got messier, not smoother.

Because none of them shared a memory. The content agent did not know what the research agent had found. The follow-up agent did not know what I had promised a client on Tuesday. Each one finished its job and remembered nothing. Every day was everyone's first day. The work simply did not compile into anything.

The agents were so forgetful that I spent more time scolding them than producing valuable work. And this is where I also learned that unlike employees, you can scold your agents and they won't resign ;)

But guess who carried all the context between them? Me. Every wire ran through me. Every brief started with me explaining the business again. Every output came back to me for the missing piece only I knew. I had added all this AI to my company and somehow given myself more to hold, not less.

Remember the octopus. I had built eight brilliant arms and no center.

If you have ever tried AI tools and quietly given up because nothing stuck together, read that again. You did not fail, and you are not bad at AI. You built arms. Nobody told you about the BRAIN. Disconnected tools forget, and forgetting is why nothing you tried ever compounded.

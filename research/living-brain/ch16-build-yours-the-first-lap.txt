CHAPTER SIXTEEN
Build yours: the first lap

Everything before this was the machine: the brain, the loop, the org chart. Do not try to build all of it this month. The first lap is deliberately small, and you already have every step:

Create the folder. Nine subfolders, from the tree in chapter ten. Ten minutes.
Set up your first agent. The Hermes steps from chapter seven, then connect your tools.
Give it one recurring job. Pick the department where your week bleeds the most hours. The goal: at least ten hours a month back within thirty days.
Make it write back. Everything it does, and what happened, goes into the folder. That is one full lap of the loop, and lap two starts smarter.

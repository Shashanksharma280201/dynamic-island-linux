# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and
not the person, and help newcomers find their way.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone, make sexual or violent remarks,
publish someone's private information, or keep pushing after someone asks you
to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent
the project. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people who
don't follow it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/shashanksharma280201). Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
